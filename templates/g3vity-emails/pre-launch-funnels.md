# G3VITY Pre-Launch Email Funnels

Five funnels that must be live before launch. Everything in [square brackets] is a placeholder to fill in or confirm.

**Placeholders to confirm before going live:** [Doctor name], [Consult length], [Price], [What's included], [Pathology provider], [Results turnaround], [Cancellation/no-show policy], [Timezone], all [Links].

---

## Funnel 1: Booking confirmation and consult prep

**Trigger:** consult booked. **Stops when:** consult happens or is cancelled.

### 1A. Instant

**Subject:** You're booked in, [First name]
**Preview:** Everything you need for your consult.

Hi [First name],

Your health assessment is booked.

**Date:** [Date]
**Time:** [Time] ([Timezone])
**Doctor:** [Doctor name]
**Join here:** [Video link]

The consult runs for about [Consult length] minutes over video. If the connection drops, we'll call you on the number you gave us, so keep your phone nearby.

Take ten minutes before your consult to get these ready:

- A list of any medications and supplements you take, including doses
- Any blood test results you already have, even if they're a few years old
- Your main concerns, written down. Energy, sleep, weight, mood, whatever brought you here. It's easy to forget the most important one once you're on the call.
- A quiet, private spot where you're comfortable talking about your health

Please fill in your health questionnaire here: [Questionnaire link]. It takes about five minutes and lets your doctor review your history before you meet, so your consult time goes on you instead of paperwork.

Need a different time? Reschedule here: [Reschedule link]. Please give us at least [24] hours notice.

See you soon,
The G3VITY team

### 2B. 24 hours after

**Subject:** The questions most people ask before booking
**Preview:** Straight answers on cost, how it works, and what happens next.

Hi [First name],

Most people who pause before booking have one of a few questions. Here are straight answers.

**How does an online consult work?**
You see a doctor by video from home. They go through your history, symptoms and goals, then decide what testing makes sense for you. If you need bloods, we send you a pathology request and you get them done at a collection centre near you.

**What does it cost?**
The health assessment is [Price]. That covers [What's included]. Pathology is billed separately by the lab, and we'll tell you about any out of pocket cost before anything is ordered.

**Is online as good as seeing someone in person?**
For this kind of assessment, the parts that matter most are a thorough history and the right blood tests. Both work just as well online. If your doctor thinks you need a physical examination, they'll tell you and point you to the right place.

**Am I just going to be sold something?**
No. The whole point of the assessment is to find out what's actually going on. Sometimes that leads to a treatment plan. Often the answer is sleep, training, nutrition, or a referral somewhere else. The plan follows your results.

Ready when you are: [Booking link]

The G3VITY team

## Funnel 4: Pathology completion

**Trigger:** pathology request sent after the first consult. **Stops when:** results are received.
This is the biggest leak in an online clinic. Track the completion rate every week.

### 4A. Instant

**Subject:** Your pathology form and how to get your bloods done
**Preview:** Three simple steps to your results.

Hi [First name],

Thanks for your consult with [Doctor name]. Your next step is a blood test.

**Your pathology request:** [Form link / "It's been sent electronically to [Pathology provider]"]

**How to get it done**

1. **Find your nearest collection centre:** [Centre finder link]. Most take walk-ins, some need a booking.
2. **Go early, and follow the instructions on your form.** For most hormone panels, that means testing before 10am after an overnight fast, with nothing but water. Testosterone is naturally highest in the morning, so a test later in the day can read low when it isn't.
3. **Bring** your form (printed or on your phone), photo ID, and your Medicare card if you have one.

Results usually take [Results turnaround] business days. We'll email you as soon as they're in so you can book your results consult.

Try to get your bloods done within the next 7 days. The sooner they're done, the sooner you have answers.

The G3VITY team

### 4B. Day 3

**Subject:** Have you had your bloods done yet?
**Preview:** Your form and nearest centre, in one place.

Hi [First name],

A quick check in. If you've already been to a collection centre, thank you. Your results are on their way and you can ignore this.

If not, here's everything you need:

- **Your form:** [Form link]
- **Nearest centre:** [Centre finder link]
- **Remember:** early morning and fasted, unless your form says otherwise

The G3VITY team

### 4C. Day 7

**Subject:** Anything stopping you getting your bloods done?
**Preview:** Most hold-ups take two minutes to fix.

If something's getting in the way, let us know. The usual hold-ups are easy to fix:

Your assessment isn't finished until we have your results. [Doctor name] can't build you a proper plan without them.

The G3VITY team

## Funnel 5: Results ready

**Trigger:** results received and reviewed by the doctor. **Stops when:** results consult booked.

> **Clinical safety rule:** any urgent or critical result is handled by the doctor phoning the patient directly. It never goes through this automated flow.

### 5A. Instant

**Subject:** Your results are in
**Preview:** Book your results consult with [Doctor name].

Hi [First name],

Your blood test results are back and [Doctor name] has reviewed them.

**Book your results consult:** [Booking link]

In this consult your doctor will go through every result with you, explain what it means for you specifically, and talk through your options from here.

To protect your privacy, we never send results by email. You can view them securely in your patient portal: [Portal link]. Numbers on their own can be misleading without context, so we'd suggest talking them through with your doctor before reading too much into any single flag.

The G3VITY team

**Subject:** Your results consult isn't booked yet
**Preview:** You've done the hard part.

Hi [First name],

Your results are ready and waiting.

You've already done the hard part with the consult and the blood test. The results consult is where it all comes together into a clear plan.

Book here: [Booking link]

Can't find a time that suits? Reply and we'll work something out.

The G3VITY team

## Setup checklist

- [ ] Funnels 1, 3, 4 and 5 are **patient care** emails. Send them from the practice management system or a platform approved for health data, and make sure marketing unsubscribes don't block them.
- [ ] Funnel 2 is **marketing**. It only moves people into lead nurture if they gave consent.
- [ ] Every email shows G3VITY as the sender and includes contact details. Marketing emails need a working unsubscribe link (Spam Act).
- [ ] No results, diagnoses or medication names in any email body. Use portal links.
- [ ] No testimonials, and no prescription treatment names in marketing emails (AHPRA/TGA).
- [ ] Test every trigger end to end with a dummy patient before launch.
- [ ] Set up team tasks: no-show call (Funnel 3), day 14 pathology call (4D), urgent results phone call (5).
