The goal & the approach

The goal is a true recomposition: build muscle and bring body fat down at the same time — not one after the other. At 98kg and 15% body fat, you're already lean enough that this works best from maintenance-to-slight-surplus calories, not a deficit. Right now you're eating ~2,300–2,500 kcal/day, sometimes less — that's under-fueling for your size and training, and it's working against the muscle you're trying to build, not for it. The fix isn't restriction, it's getting enough food in consistently — built from four foods you already eat without issue: steak, mince, eggs, and protein shakes, plus rice and potato for carbs.

Your day, meal by meal

Four meals, same structure every day — simple on purpose, so it's easy to actually hit.

Breakfast
4 whole eggs + ½ avocado + protein shake~50g protein, ~530 kcal. Sets up the day without needing carbs this early.
Lunch
Steak (250g) cooked in olive oil + rice (350g)~82g protein, ~98g carbs, ~1,050 kcal. Main training-fuel meal of the day.
Dinner
Beef mince (250g) cooked in olive oil + potato (450g)~74g protein, ~90g carbs, ~1,135 kcal. Biggest meal — replenishes what the day's training used.
Snack
2 whole eggs + protein shake~38g protein, ~265 kcal. Closes the protein target out, easy to fit in wherever it works.

6

The flexible-meal fix

Pasta swap, not a cheat meal When rice or potato gets repetitive, swap in pasta at roughly the same cooked weight (300g pasta ≈ 350g rice for carbs) — same targets, less monotony. If a takeout meal happens, hit protein through eggs/shake as normal earlier in the day and let takeout cover the rest. Planned flexibility is sustainable; total rigidity usually isn't.
7

Building up, not jumping in

Going from ~2,300–2,500 kcal straight to 2,950 overnight is a 20%+ jump — you'll feel overly full, adherence will drop, and you'll likely bail back to old habits within a week. Step it up instead:

  • Week 1 — ~2,600 kcal: add the snack (2 eggs + shake) to your current eating.
  • Week 2 — ~2,800 kcal: bring the rice and potato portions up to full size.
  • Week 3 onward — ~2,950 kcal: full plan, olive oil and avocado included.

This isn't just about comfort — consistently under-eating relative to training load is the mechanism behind Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (RED-S), the IOC's consensus term for chronic low energy availability. It blunts recovery and hormonal function, which directly works against building muscle. The ramp-up gets you to an adequate intake without your gut vetoing the plan in week one.

8

Timeline & how to adjust

Recomposition is slower than a pure bulk or pure cut — it's asking the body to do two things at once. Set expectations at 12–16+ weeks, not a fast transformation.

Monitoring Weigh in 3x/week (same conditions), track the weekly average. Waist measurement every 2 weeks. Photos every 2–4 weeks.

Reassess every 2–3 weeks: if weight/waist aren't trending and lifts are climbing, this is working — hold. If waist keeps rising with no strength gains, trim 150–200 kcal from carbs first. If strength starts dropping or you're losing weight fast, add calories back — that's muscle loss, not fat loss, and it undoes the whole point of this plan.
