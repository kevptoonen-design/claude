Why these numbers

99 to 91.5 kg over 8 weeks is about 0.95 kg a week, roughly 1% of bodyweight. That sits at the top of the 0.5 to 1% per week range Helms and colleagues found best for holding onto muscle while dieting. Aggressive, but manageable if protein and lifting stay locked in.

Expect week 1 to drop faster than target. That's mostly water and glycogen. Expect a stall week somewhere in the middle too. If the weekly average misses target two weeks running, trim around 150 to 200 kcal from carbs or fats. Protein stays at 200 g.

Targets finish on Sun 29 Nov so week 9 is a buffer and hold week before 5 Dec. Keep your bloodwork and test plan with your doctor. This page only tracks that you did it.
